# 📱 FAST NUCES SGPA Calculator

An intuitive and efficient Android application built to help **FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES)** students easily calculate their Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA). 

Designed as a semester project, this app removes the hassle of manual calculations by strictly adhering to the official **FAST NUCES relative/absolute grading policy** and credit hour structures.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Official FAST Grading Policy:** Pre-configured with FAST NUCES letter grades and their corresponding GPA points (e.g., A+, A, A-, B+, B, etc.).
- 📊 **SGPA Calculation:** Input course marks/grades alongside credit hours to generate accurate semester results instantly.
- 📈 **CGPA Tracker:** Calculate overall CGPA across multiple semesters by factoring in total earned credits and current GPA.
- ➕ **Dynamic Course Input:** Easily add or remove subjects dynamically based on your semester workload.
- 🎨 **Clean & Modern UI:** Simple, responsive interface built with Android design guidelines for fast and effortless navigation.
- 💾 **Local Data Storage:** Save your semester records locally on your device to track your academic progress over time.
